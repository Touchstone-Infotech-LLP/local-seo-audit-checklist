## Local ranking concepts

Google describes local results using three broad concepts:

- **Relevance** — how closely a business matches what someone is searching for.
- **Distance** — how far the business is from the location used or implied in the search.
- **Prominence** — how well-known and established the business appears to be.

This checklist focuses on the parts a business can realistically influence: profile accuracy, category fit, website quality, reviews, local authority, citations, content, technical quality and operational consistency.

No checklist can override geographic distance or guarantee a map-pack ranking.

## Recommended workflow

**Verify → Complete → Align → Strengthen → Measure → Maintain**

1. Verify the business profile and ownership.
2. Complete all important GBP fields accurately.
3. Align GBP, website, citations and real-world business details.
4. Strengthen reviews, content, links and local proof.
5. Measure calls, website visits, directions and qualified leads.
6. Maintain the profile continuously instead of treating optimization as a one-time task.

## Scoring

Use the following model for applicable checks:

- **2** — Pass
- **1** — Needs improvement
- **0** — Fail
- **N/A** — Not applicable

| Score | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| 90–100% | Strong local SEO foundation |
| 75–89% | Good, with improvement opportunities |
| 50–74% | Significant gaps remain |
| Below 50% | High-priority local SEO work required |

A score is only a prioritization aid. A single serious issue such as a suspended profile, wrong address, incorrect category or duplicate listing can outweigh many low-priority passes.
