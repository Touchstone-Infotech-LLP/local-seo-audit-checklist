Understand local rankings before optimizing

Local visibility cannot be reduced to a single tactic. Think in terms of three broad concepts.

Relevance

How closely the business and its information match what a searcher is looking for.

Distance

How far the business is from the location used or implied by the search. This is not something SEO can simply override.

Prominence

How established and well-known the business appears through its real-world and online presence.

Avoid ranking guarantees. Accurate profiles, relevant categories, strong websites, genuine reviews, citations and local authority can improve your foundation, but no checklist guarantees map-pack placement.
