# Contributing

Contributions to the Local SEO & Google Business Profile Audit Checklist are welcome.

## Useful contributions

Examples include:

- corrections where Google Business Profile functionality or policy has changed,
- improvements to audit wording,
- missing checks with clear practical value,
- accessibility and implementation improvements,
- clearer explanations for local business owners,
- country-specific notes that do not reduce the checklist's global usefulness.

## Contribution principles

Please keep proposed changes:

1. **Vendor-neutral** — avoid promoting software solely for commercial reasons.
2. **Evidence-oriented** — distinguish documented platform guidance from observation or opinion.
3. **Globally useful** — clearly label country-specific behavior where relevant.
4. **Policy-safe** — do not recommend fake reviews, keyword-stuffed business names, fake locations, review gating, fabricated citations or other deceptive tactics.
5. **Practical** — each checklist item should be reasonably auditable and actionable.
6. **Non-guaranteed** — do not claim that a single tactic guarantees local rankings or map-pack placement.

## How to contribute

Open an issue describing the improvement or submit a pull request with the proposed change.

For larger structural changes, an issue first is preferred so the scope can be discussed.
